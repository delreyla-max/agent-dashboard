How we personalize your job search
Four signals help us find better matches for you.
1 Resume Keywords
We extract skills, tools, and keywords from your resume to improve search relevance.
2 Past Titles & Roles
We use your previous job titles and roles to understand the kinds of jobs that fit your experience.
3 Your Activity
We learn from what you save, skip, and thumbs down to refine your results over time.
4 Future Fit Prediction
We predict what you may be looking for next and surface opportunities before you search for them.
Your data is private and secure. We never share it.
