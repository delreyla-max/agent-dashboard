When evaluating job postings against this profile, score each on a 0–100 scale using this rubric:

**Role level match (25 points)**
Full 25: VP Research, VP UX Research, Head of Research, Head of UX Research — with explicit team leadership scope.
15–20: Senior Director or Director Research/UX Research — with manage-managers scope or team of 5+.
5–10: Director with unclear scope, Principal Researcher with strategic influence but no direct reports.
0: IC-only researcher, Research Manager (no manager-of-managers), contract/temp.

**Core skill overlap (30 points)**
Score based on how many of Chris's core areas the role requires:
- Research org building / scaling (max 10)
- Mixed methods, discovery, information-seeking, recommendation/personalization research (max 10)
- AI/ML product research, trust, mental models, algorithmic UX (max 5)
- Executive influence, cross-functional research strategy (max 5)

**Domain fit (15 points)**
Full 15: Consumer apps, social platforms, marketplaces, dating/relationship tech, fintech.
10–12: Tech platforms with search, discovery, ranking, or recommendation systems; AI/ML product companies.
5–8: Enterprise SaaS, B2B platforms with some consumer-facing research.
0–4: Healthcare, government, agency/consulting without product research focus, pure market research.

**Differentiator leverage (15 points)**
Does the role specifically reward:
- Building or scaling a research org from scratch or through major growth (max 6)
- AI/ML UX research, trust, or mental models expertise (max 5)
- Global/international research at scale (max 4)

**Risk and gaps (subtract up to 15 points)**
Deduct for:
- Role appears IC-heavy despite director title (-5 to -10)
- Domain mismatch (e.g., primarily market research, not product UX) (-5 to -10)
- Compensation clearly below $180K if stated (-5 to -15)
- Seniority ambiguity — "Manager" title without confirmed manage-managers scope (-5)
- Relocation required without flagging it (-3)
